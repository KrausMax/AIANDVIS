The use of AI greatly speed up the process of initial setup so I was able to provide the team a first look at the data very quickly. We were able to decide on how to move forward quicker and had a general idea of how the dataset/model functioned.

For the Visualizations it was all over the place without proper guidance on what to visualize, initial visualizations showed some general themes. 
However it was important to then focus the LLM on the parts of the data/types of visualizations that were interesting to investigate.

Particularly for the Interactive Visualizer it was very helpful. I do not think I would have tried to implement an Interactive component if the usage of LLMs was not permitted.
It also allowed me to explore several options before actual implementation by simply prompting to give a few examples of how such a Visualization could look/be implemented.

My approach was: 
1)  Initial exploration of topics of interest (no AI) 
2)  First implementation of Data/Model/Visualizations (AI)
3)  understanding the data, model, error behaviours (no AI)
4) implementation of specialized Visualizations for the project (AI)
5) finding insights/discussion, preparing presentation (no AI)
